Why not just use stars?

One person's four stars is another person's five. Pile up enough ratings and every decent place ends up at 4.5.

Star ratings

  • You have to decide on a number right after eating
  • Everyone grades on a different curve
  • Good places all cluster at the top, so scores stop meaning much

Shiji

  • You only describe what you liked and what you didn't
  • Each new place is compared with restaurants you've already been to
  • Your rank becomes a score, so your standards stay consistent
